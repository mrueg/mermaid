Complex Example — E-Commerce Platform

Four interactions across a realistic topology: the Dev Platform provides services to two stream teams via X-as-a-Service; the QA Enablement team facilitates the Cart team; and the Payment Gateway collaborates with the Checkout team. Each interaction is a separate chain (team ~~~ connector ~~~ team). Connector shapes carry no text label — the shape and color identify the interaction mode.
