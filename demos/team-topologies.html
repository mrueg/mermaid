Team Topologies Shapes

Colors match the official Team Topologies shape templates. Interaction modes (Collaboration, Facilitation, X-as-a-Service) appear as connector shapes on the line between two teams — not as endpoint nodes. They use 50% fill-opacity to convey their transient nature per Team Topologies design guidelines.
