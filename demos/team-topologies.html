Team Topologies Shapes

Colors match the official Team Topologies shape templates. Interaction modes (Collaboration, Facilitation, X-as-a-Service) appear as connector shapes between two teams, overlapping each team node on both sides to match the official Team Topologies visual convention. They use 50% fill-opacity to convey their transient nature.

For X-as-a-Service the base (left side) of the triangle connects to the provider and the tip (right side) connects to the consumer.

Collaboration (interaction mode connector)

Complicated Subsystem team collaborates with Stream-aligned team. The parallelogram shape sits between them with no connecting lines.

X-as-a-Service (interaction mode connector)

Platform team (provider) offers a service consumed by Stream-aligned team (consumer). Base of triangle ← provider. Tip of triangle → consumer.
